## Required Review

- Activation legality: Optional Ignition Effect of a face-up "3-Hump Lacooda" controlled by the turn player. In the current GOAT priority model this can be activated in that player's Main Phase 1 or Main Phase 2 while the gamestate is open and they control exactly 3 face-up "3-Hump Lacooda" monsters.
- Costs: Tribute 2 of those face-up "3-Hump Lacooda" monsters as the activation cost. The activating copy may be one of the two Tributed copies, or it may be the copy left on the field.
- Targets: None. The Tributed monsters are costs, not targets.
- Chain timing / spell speed: Spell Speed 1 monster Ignition Effect that starts a Chain after the cost is paid. If the opponent chains "Ring of Destruction" or "Book of Moon", the draw still resolves; the effect does not require the activating copy to remain face-up on the field. Under Goat-era Ignition priority, after Summoning the third copy the controller can activate this before the opponent activates "Torrential Tribute".
- Trigger timing / missed timing: Not a Trigger Effect. There is no mandatory trigger and no missed-timing issue; the player simply chooses whether to activate the Ignition Effect while legal.
- Continuous or replacement behavior: None.
- Battle and Damage Step behavior: No battle modifier or battle trigger. It cannot be activated during the Battle Phase or Damage Step because it is Spell Speed 1 Ignition-only.
- Edge cases: Face-down copies do not count. Opponent-controlled copies do not count for "your side of the field". The cost must be two distinct controlled face-up copies. If the player has fewer than 3 cards left in Deck, drawing proceeds card by card and normal deck-out handling applies.

## Implementation Summary

Added a field-effect activation action for implemented field effects, a script context cost list, and a tribute-cost helper. Implemented "3-Hump Lacooda" as an exact-three face-up controlled copy check that Tributes two selected copies as cost, then draws 3 cards.

## Acceptance Tests

- Activating one face-up copy while controlling exactly three copies can Tribute the activating copy plus one other copy and draw 3 cards, leaving the third copy on the field.
- Activation is rejected without drawing or Tributing when the third copy is face-down.
